Just the games

  • No ads

    None. No banners, no videos to sit through between levels.

  • No account

    Open it and play. No sign-up, no email, no profile.

  • No internet

    Every level is on your phone. Play on a plane or in the subway.

  • A free game every day

    The game of the day is the same for everyone, and it is always free.

Seven games, one app

Each one with three difficulties, and hints for when you get stuck.

Arrow Crossword

The clues sit inside the grid, each with an arrow that shows where the answer goes.

Classic Crossword

Fill the grid with the answers to the clues, across and down.

Word Search

Find every word on the list hidden in the grid.

Letter Wheel

Connect the letters on the wheel to make every word on the board.

Guess the Word

Guess the five-letter word in six tries or fewer.

A to Z

Go around the alphabet: one clue for each letter.

Sudoku

Fill every row, column and 3 × 3 box with the numbers 1 to 9, no repeats.

A free game, every day

  1. MonGuess the Word

  4. ThuLetter Wheel
  5. FriGuess the Word
  6. SatArrow Crossword
  7. SunClassic Crossword

Also free: the first two Easy levels of every game. Ovilo Pro unlocks every level, the archive of earlier days and unlimited hints.
